Implementation:

- Two bounded recovery attempts for nonfatal failures during cycling.
- Fatal TIMEOUT/NO_DEVICE/PIPE latches: zero later USB calls on that handle.
- Response deadline2seconds plus at most one already-started1secondUSBtransfer.
